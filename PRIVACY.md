# Privacy

- Address and station search terms and the use filter stay in the browser.
- Compared entries contain only public standard-point data and are limited to four records in local storage.
- Telemetry stores only an allowlisted event name, one-way session hash, QA flag, and timestamp.
- Search terms, prefecture, point IDs, coordinates, contact data, advertising identifiers, and authentication data are not collected as product events.
- Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control opt out of product telemetry.
- Telemetry expires after 35 days; automated QA is excluded from human metrics.
